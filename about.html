About CGPA Calculator BD

CGPA Calculator BD is a free online educational tool designed to help university students calculate CGPA, convert marks into grades, and understand the CGPA they may need to achieve their academic targets.

What We Provide

Our website provides simple and easy-to-use academic calculators, including:

  • CGPA Calculator
  • Marks to Grade Calculator
  • Target CGPA Calculator
  • University-based grading information
  • Useful academic calculation tools

Our Goal

Our goal is to make academic calculations easier for students in Bangladesh. Instead of calculating CGPA manually, students can use simple online tools and get results quickly.

Accuracy

We aim to provide accurate calculations and useful grading information. However, grading policies can change between universities and academic periods. Students should always verify official grading policies with their respective university when making important academic decisions.

Free Educational Resource

CGPA Calculator BD is intended as an educational and calculation resource. The calculators are provided for convenience and should not replace official university academic records or policies.
