About CGPA Calculator BD

Simple, useful and student-friendly academic calculation tools for university students in Bangladesh.

What is CGPA Calculator BD?

CGPA Calculator BD is a free online academic calculation platform designed to help university students in Bangladesh calculate their GPA and CGPA more easily.

Instead of doing complicated calculations manually, students can use our tools to calculate their semester GPA, cumulative CGPA, required target GPA and marks-to-grade results.

The goal is to make academic calculations simple, fast and easy to understand for students.

What You Can Do

🎓

CGPA Calculator

Calculate your semester GPA and cumulative CGPA based on course credits and grades.

📊

Marks → Grade

Enter your marks and find the corresponding letter grade and grade point.

🎯

Target CGPA

Calculate the GPA you may need in future courses to reach your target CGPA.

Universities

CGPA Calculator BD is being developed to support grading systems used by universities in Bangladesh.

Our goal is to provide useful tools for both public and private university students.

  • Public university students
  • Private university students
  • Different university grading scales
  • Credit-based GPA and CGPA calculations
  • Marks-to-grade conversion

Why We Built This

GPA and CGPA calculations can become confusing when students have different course credits, grades and university grading systems.

CGPA Calculator BD aims to provide a simple interface where students can enter their academic information and get an easy-to-understand result.

We are continuously working to improve the tools, university coverage and overall user experience.

Important Note

CGPA Calculator BD is an independent educational utility website. Results generated by the calculators are intended for informational and calculation purposes.

Students should verify their official grades, credits and grading policies with their respective university before using a calculated result for official purposes.
