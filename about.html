About CGPA Calculator BD

CGPA Calculator BD is a free online academic tool designed to help university students in Bangladesh calculate GPA, CGPA and target academic results easily.

What is CGPA Calculator BD?

CGPA Calculator BD is a simple and easy-to-use website for students who want to calculate their semester GPA, overall CGPA and required GPA for a future target.

The website is designed with Bangladesh university students in mind and aims to make academic calculations faster and easier.

Our Academic Tools

  • CGPA Calculator
  • Marks to Grade Converter
  • Target CGPA Calculator
  • University-based academic calculation tools

How It Works

Select your university, enter your course credits and grades, and the calculator will calculate the required result based on the information you provide.

Always compare calculated results with your university's official academic rules when making important academic decisions.
