About This CGPA Calculator

CGPA Calculator BD is a free online calculator designed for Bangladesh university students. You can calculate your current semester GPA and, when previous academic information is entered, estimate your overall CGPA.

This calculator is intended as an educational tool. For official academic results, always follow your university's published grading policy and transcript.
