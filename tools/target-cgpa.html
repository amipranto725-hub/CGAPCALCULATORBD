How the Calculation Works

Required GPA = [Target CGPA × (Completed Credits + Future Credits) − Current CGPA × Completed Credits] ÷ Future Credits

Example

Suppose:

  • Current CGPA = 3.10
  • Completed Credits = 90
  • Target CGPA = 3.50
  • Future Credits = 30

Required GPA: 4.70

In this example, the target is mathematically impossible because the required future GPA is greater than the maximum GPA of 4.00.

Important Information

The calculator assumes that your current CGPA and completed credits are accurate and that all future credits contribute normally to your CGPA. University policies regarding repeated courses, grade replacement, retakes, withdrawn courses, transfer credits and excluded courses may affect the actual CGPA.
