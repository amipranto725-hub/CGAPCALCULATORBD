About CGPA Calculator BD

CGPA Calculator BD is a simple educational tool designed for university students in Bangladesh. It helps students calculate updated CGPA, understand course grade points and estimate the GPA required to achieve a target CGPA.


The calculator supports multiple public and private universities and uses university-specific grading scales where available.
