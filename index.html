How to Calculate CGPA

  1. Enter your previous CGPA.
  2. Enter the total credits you have already completed.
  3. Add each current semester course with its credit and grade.
  4. Click the Calculate CGPA button.
  5. The calculator will show your semester GPA and updated overall CGPA.

CGPA Formula

CGPA is calculated using the total quality points divided by the total completed credits.
